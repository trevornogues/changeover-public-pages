Changelog

What shipped, and when. Newest first.

1.1 September 25, 2026

Changeover AI is on. It starts with two free weeks, then $14.99 a year, and you can try it before you decide whether it's worth it.

  • Insights. What you're doing well, the pattern that keeps showing up, the mental pattern, and what to watch for, read from your journal, not from the internet.
  • Ask Changeover. One question, one answer from your own history. “Why do I keep losing close matches?” is a good first one.
  • Free trial. Two weeks free for eligible Apple IDs. Cancel during the trial and you're not charged.
  • The app itself is free. Everything in the journal stays free, and your notes stay on this device.
  • The app now asks for an App Store review at a quiet moment. If you like it, that helps a solo project more than you'd think.

1.0 September 21, 2026

First release on the App Store.

  • Log a match, a practice, or a hit while it's still fresh: set-by-set score, how it felt, what worked, what didn't, what to remember for next time. Put this weekend's match on the schedule too.
  • Last time vs. Type an opponent you've played before and the last thing you wrote about them comes right back.
  • History with filters and month summaries.
  • Trends, Patterns, streak, and Competition, all counted on your phone. “You've mentioned your second serve in 6 of your last 9 match notes.”
  • Equipment. Rackets, shoes, and strings in the bag, setups with tension and strung date, and hours on the string bed.
  • Share cards. A clean card or a transparent sticker for a good session or a good month.
  • Reminders, opt-in, scheduled on your phone.
  • Three colour schemes (Centre Lawn, Clubhouse, Blazer) in light and dark.
  • No account. No feed. The journal lives on your phone.
